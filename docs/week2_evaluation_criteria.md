# Week 2 Evaluation Criteria — SmartPark AI

**Version:** 1.0  
**Basis:** Week 1 project scope and Week 2 Sections 1–3 (Functional, Non-Functional and Responsible AI Requirements).

The following criteria select important, testable requirements from the existing requirements specification. The thresholds are proposed acceptance targets for the miniature parking prototype; they are not reported results. Objective links refer to the Week 1 objectives: **O1** parking-bay occupancy detection, **O2** vehicle tracking and basic guidance, and **O3** evaluation of detection and tracking/guidance performance.

| Objective / Requirement ID | Evaluation Criterion | Capability Bucket | Measurement Method | Metric | Threshold | User / Business Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **O1, O3** — FR1, NFR2, RAI1 | **Parking-bay occupancy correctness:** assess whether the system correctly identifies occupied and vacant bays and avoids reporting an occupied bay as vacant. | **Domain-specific capability** | Use at least 100 held-out labelled frames containing both occupied and vacant bays. Compare every bay prediction with the manual ground truth. Also review at least 100 labelled occupied-bay observations for false-vacant results. | Macro-F1 across occupied/vacant classes; number of false-vacant events. | **Macro-F1 ≥ 0.90** and **0 false-vacant events** in at least 100 occupied-bay observations. | Drivers are less likely to be directed to an occupied bay, and operators receive more dependable occupancy information. |
| **O2, O3** — FR2, NFR3, RAI2 | **Vehicle-tracking consistency and uncertainty handling:** assess whether the system keeps the correct vehicle track across frames and withholds position-dependent guidance when a track is lost, mismatched or unconfirmed. | **Domain-specific capability** | Test at least 20 annotated video clips containing normal movement, temporary occlusion, vehicles passing close to one another, and vehicles leaving/re-entering the camera view. Compare track assignments with manual annotations and inspect all guidance events. | Correct track-association rate; number of guidance events based on incorrect, mismatched or unconfirmed tracks. | **Correct track-association rate ≥ 90%** and **0 guidance events** based on incorrect, mismatched or unconfirmed tracks across the test clips. | Users receive more dependable vehicle-position information and are less likely to follow guidance based on a wrongly tracked vehicle. |
| **O2, O3** — FR4, FR5 | **Parking availability and guidance correctness:** assess whether the system selects a bay recorded as vacant, reports when no vacant bay is identified, and displays the correct bay identifier and location or direction. | **Domain-specific capability** | Run at least 20 controlled layout scenarios, including scenarios with one or more vacant bays and scenarios where all bays are occupied. Compare the selected bay, availability message and displayed guidance with the known layout and ground truth. | Correct availability decisions and guidance outputs divided by total scenarios; number of occupied-bay recommendations. | **At least 19 of 20 scenarios (≥ 95%)** must have a correct availability decision and matching guidance; **0 occupied bays** may be recommended. | Drivers can identify a suitable vacant bay more easily, while operators avoid presenting unavailable spaces as options. |
| **O1, O2, O3** — NFR1 | **Processing latency:** assess whether the system produces occupancy and vehicle-position results quickly enough for use in the miniature demonstration. | **Cost and latency** | Record the time each of at least 100 test frames is received and the time its corresponding occupancy and tracking result becomes available, using one documented test setup. | 95th-percentile frame-to-result latency, in seconds. | **≤ 2.0 seconds** for the 95th-percentile latency. | Users receive parking status and guidance without excessive waiting during the demonstration. |
| **O1, O2, O3** — NFR4 | **Operational reliability:** assess whether the prototype completes repeated test sequences without crashing, losing its input stream or failing to provide an output. | **Domain-specific capability** | Run at least 20 repeatable sequences covering vehicle movement, bay entry/exit, occupancy updates and availability/guidance output. Record each completed run and any listed failure. | Successful test-run rate: completed runs without a crash, input loss or missing output divided by total runs. | **At least 19 of 20 runs (≥ 95%)** complete without a listed failure. | Users and operators can complete a demonstration or test session without frequent interruptions or missing results. |

## Evaluation Notes

- The selected criteria cover the project's core domain capability, responsible output handling, operational reliability and latency. The **Generation capability** and **Instruction-following capability** buckets are not used because SmartPark AI is a computer-vision parking prototype, not a text-generation or conversational instruction-following application.
- RAI3 (privacy) remains a requirement in Section 3 of `week2_requirements.md`. It should be checked through the privacy/data-retention review specified there; it is not forced into an unrelated capability bucket in this task-focused evaluation table.
- Use held-out images and clips that were not used to tune or develop the system. Keep the manual labels, test scenarios and measured results as evidence.
- Record the camera, processing device, input resolution and lighting conditions for each test. If the setup changes materially, repeat the evaluation.
- Thresholds are proposed acceptance targets. Report actual measured values honestly and do not claim that SmartPark AI has met a threshold until it has been tested.
